What is a HipoBuySpreadsheet?

A curated directory of Weidian, Taobao and 1688 links you paste into a shopping agent.

Short definition

A HipoBuySpreadsheet is community shorthand for a browsable collection of Chinese marketplace product links. You discover an item, open the live seller page, then paste that URL into Hipobuy.

What HipoBuySpreadsheet adds

HipoBuySpreadsheet turns the old slow-sheet habit into category hubs and product HTML pages that search engines can index.

What it is not

It is not Hipobuy itself, not a payment processor, and not a guarantee of stock. Always re-check the live page and warehouse QC.
