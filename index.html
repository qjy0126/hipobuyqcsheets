FAQ

What is a HipoBuy QC sheet?

A category-based reference: inspection notes plus catalog rows with prices and marketplace links. You shortlist here, then buy through a shopping agent.

What is a HipoBuy QC photo?

Warehouse photos taken after an item arrives at the agent. Use them to inspect logos, shape and defects before you approve international shipping.

How does HipoBuy QC Finder work?

Search a product name, brand or numeric item ID. Matching records in this archive open with QC photos and source links.

Is HipoBuySpreadsheet the official HipoBuy site?

No. This is an independent directory at hipobuyqcsheets.com. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by HipoBuy.

Does this website sell products?

No. Checkout, warehouse QC and shipping happen on HipoBuy or another agent you choose.
