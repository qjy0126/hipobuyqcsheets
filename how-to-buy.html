How to buy

Shortlist on HipoBuySpreadsheet, order through an agent, then GL only after QC photos look right.

1. Find the item

Use QC Finder or the spreadsheet catalog. Open the live Weidian / Taobao / 1688 listing and confirm stock and price.

2. Paste into your agent

Copy the source URL into HipoBuy (or CNFans, Kakobuy, Mulebuy, Sugargoo). Pay the item cost to warehouse.

3. Review warehouse QC

Inspect logos, shape, stitching and defects in the photos. Request remakes when something is off.

4. Ship internationally

Compare lines by weight and destination, pay freight, then track the parcel.
