Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

hipobuyqcsheets.com (“the site”, “we”) is an independent visual index of Taobao, Weidian, and 1688 finds. We do not sell products, take payment, or ship parcels. Checkout, warehouse QC, and shipping happen on Hipobuy or another agent you choose.

Information we collect

This catalog is a static website. We do not create user accounts, and we do not ask you to submit a name, email, or payment card to browse the spreadsheet.

Like most sites, our host or CDN may automatically receive technical data when you load a page — for example IP address, browser type, referring URL, and which pages were requested. That data is used to operate, secure, and debug the site, not to sell a profile of you.

Cookies and analytics

We do not use advertising cookies. If a future analytics or security tool sets a cookie, it will only be to understand aggregate traffic or to keep the site working. You can block cookies in your browser; the catalog will still open.

Third-party links

Product cards open Weidian, Taobao, 1688, and Hipobuy (or other agents). Those sites have their own privacy policies. Once you leave hipobuyqcsheets.com, we do not control what they collect.

Fonts may load from Google Fonts. Google’s privacy terms apply to that request.

Product photos

Listing images come from the shared catalog used to build this index. They are shown so you can preview a find before you open the live source page. We do not claim ownership of brand logos or seller photos.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy when the site changes. The date at the top is the latest revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the About page. For orders, QC photos, or parcels, contact Hipobuy or the agent that holds your warehouse goods — we cannot see or change agent accounts.
